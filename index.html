How the AI Makes Decisions

SafeLearn uses live sensor information and predefined safety rules to interpret the environment. The system does not need a camera or facial recognition.

🌡 Temperature Normal conditions are monitored continuously. Higher temperatures increase the safety level.
💧 Humidity High humidity can cause the system to recommend monitoring or taking a break.
🔊 Noise Higher noise levels can trigger a recommendation to move to a quieter study environment.
💡 Light Low light can trigger a recommendation to improve the study area's lighting.
🏃 Activity Low activity can trigger a movement-break suggestion.
🚨 Safety priority Multiple unsafe readings can raise the overall system status to DANGER.
